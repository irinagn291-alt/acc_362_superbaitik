SuperBaitik is an English-language, offline period diary. All features are available on first launch. No account, purchase, special permissions, or network connection is required.

To try the calendar and estimates, open Today and choose Log a period. Enter a first day about five weeks ago, turn on Period has ended, and choose a last day a few days later. Save. Add a second period starting about one week ago. Today now shows an estimated next start. Open Insights to compare the start-to-start interval with recorded period lengths. Tap an interval under Cycle length to open its Cycle recap. Check-ins appear beside their day within that cycle. You can edit or delete a period from Journal.

Open Daily check-in from Today or choose a past date in Calendar. Enter an energy level, cramp level, or note. Save, reopen, and select the day in Check-in history to edit or delete it.

Today > Guide explains the estimate with a worked example. The arithmetic mean of the most recent one to six recorded intervals is rounded to a whole day and added to the latest start. SuperBaitik is intended for personal recordkeeping and planning. It does not predict ovulation, provide contraception guidance, or make diagnoses.

The privacy policy is available from Today and the Guide. Journal entries are processed on this device and excluded from backups.
