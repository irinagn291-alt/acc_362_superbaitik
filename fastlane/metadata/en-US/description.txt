SuperBaitik is an English-language diary for recording periods and daily observations.

Log a period’s first day, add its last day when known, and keep an optional note. Use daily check-ins to record energy, cramps, and personal notes for today or a past day.

Explore your entries in a monthly calendar or journal. Insights show recorded cycle and period lengths, with recaps that bring a cycle’s dates and check-ins together. After at least two period starts, the app can estimate your next start from recent recorded intervals.

Estimates are for personal planning. They cannot determine ovulation or safe days for contraception. Your entries are stored on your device.
